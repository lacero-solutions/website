What we do

The challenge as we observe it

The global Takata airbag and seatbelt recall has adversely impacted the automotive sector and its recycling and sustainability goals. Globally, more than 100 million defective airbags have been recalled as of 2022 — about 70 million in North America alone. Additionally, about 12 million vehicles reach end of life each year, mostly ending up in scrapyards and landfills, generating huge airbag waste that could instead be recycled for its components.

Non-Takata recalls have also steadily averaged around 1 million units a year, creating a burgeoning airbag waste management challenge for OEMs.

Our innovative solutions

Safe, secure, zero-waste disposal

Lacero Solutions is an industry leader in safely disposing of any defective or recalled airbags — all airbag inflators including Takata, full airbag modules and seatbelt tensioners. Our secure, customized solutions ensure these units are diverted from landfills and every component is recycled safely.

Our electro-mechanical waste disposal process ensures no wastewater discharge and minimal air-impact emissions. We give clients the tools to achieve their recycling goals and ensure a safe environment for future generations.

Our services

Airbag & seatbelt pretensioner disposal and recycling

We are set up to safely dispose of scrap, defective and recalled airbag modules, inflators and seatbelt pretensioners.

Safe deactivation of recalled airbags

Our facilities can destruct any recalled or defective airbags in a safe, eco-friendly manner. We are a zero-waste facility with no wastewater discharge and minimal-impact air emissions. 100% of airbags are destroyed and all hazardous waste is treated per regulation.

Safe deployment & recycling of seatbelt pretensioners

We are permitted to receive, handle and safely deploy recalled or defective seatbelt pretensioners. Our program ensures proper handling of hazardous waste and recovers recyclable materials.

Customized recycling of full modules, struts, boosters & driveshafts

We receive and dispose of all airbag module types — side, side curtain and knee — and assist with specialized disposal of struts, brake boosters, driveshafts and other hard-to-recycle solid hazardous manufacturing waste.

Comprehensive barcode tracking & destruction certificates

Our in-house IMS/WMS provides real-time tracking of your airbags and seatbelts. We issue a destruction certificate for each unit, ensuring none of your units reach the secondary market — protecting reputation and intellectual property.

Secured facility for storage & pick-up services

Store your airbags and inflators in our secured, certified facility before processing, removing the need for large on-site storage. We also provide pick-up and delivery for safe, seamless transport to our facility.
