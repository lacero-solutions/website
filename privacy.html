Lacero Solutions Inc. – Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 24th, 2025

Overview

Lacero Solutions Inc., a corporation duly incorporated under the laws of the Province of Ontario ("Lacero", "We", "Us" or "Our"), operates this website with the URL of https://www.lacerosolutions.com/ (this "Website"). This Privacy Policy (this "Policy") governs Our practices with respect to the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information collected on this Website.

This Policy

This Policy demonstrates Our commitment to protecting Your privacy and is incorporated into Our Terms of Use. We may use Your personal information to provide and improve this Website and Our services. By using this Website, You agree to the collection and use of personal information in accordance with this Policy.

Collecting and Using Your Personal Data

This Website collects various types of personal information that is personally identifiable to You and may include Your name, e-mail address, and phone number, among other types of personal information. Collection of personal information through this Website will not be excessive and We will not ask for information, such as social security numbers, banking details, date of birth, etc.

On a voluntary basis, You may provide and disclose personal information through this Website through the Contact link to receive Lacero news and Lacero information. From time to time, We may use personal information to assist in the operation of this Website and to ensure quality of its contents. We will not disclose or sell any personal information that You provide through this Website without Your permission, unless required to do so by law.

We may collect anonymous information about You, meaning that certain information collected cannot be traced back to a specific person. This information is collected using "cookies" and might include the pages visited, IP addresses and other Website usage statistics.

In some cases, We may also use cookies provided by trusted third parties to help Us understand how visitors use this Website and find ways We can improve Your experience using the Website. These cookies may track things such as time spent on the Website and pages visited on the Website.

Security

We will treat Your personal information as private, confidential information and We will make a conscientious effort to ensure that this personal information is protected. We will be diligent in Our efforts to protect Your personal information; however, as data transmission over the Internet is not secure, We cannot guarantee that such information will not be lost, used unlawfully or modified in a fraudulent manner.

External Links

This Website may contain links that take You to external websites elsewhere on the Internet. This statement does not extend to the data collection practices of third parties, and We do not assume any responsibility for the privacy practices, policies or actions of third parties. We recommend reading such third-party privacy policies to make an informed decision as to whether or not You wish to use these sites based on their privacy practices, especially in the event such third parties ask You to submit personal information.

Changes to this Policy

We reserve the right to change this Policy when it deems necessary and without prior notice or consent. Any changes made to this Privacy will be in compliance with privacy laws in effect in Canada and will be posted on this page of this Website.

Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 13. If You are a parent or guardian and You are aware that Your child has provided Us with personal information, please contact Us. If We become aware that We have collected personal information from anyone under the age of 13 without verification of parental or guardian consent, We will take steps to remove that information from Our servers. If We need to rely on consent as a legal basis for processing Your information and Your country requires consent from a parent or guardian, We may require Your parent or guardian's consent before We collect and use that information.

Contact Us
